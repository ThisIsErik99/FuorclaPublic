Contact

Before you write

To help me solve the issue faster, please include:

  • Your iOS version and iPhone model
  • The Fuorcla version (visible in Settings)
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead

Common questions

  • Fuorcla doesn't show my rides. Check that HealthKit access is allowed in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Fuorcla.
  • Photos aren't appearing on the map. Fuorcla matches photos automatically by time and location. Make sure Photos access is granted and that Location Services were on when the photo was taken.
  • Segments aren't backed up. Segment backup uses your personal iCloud account. Make sure you're signed into iCloud and have enough iCloud storage available.

For details on what data Fuorcla accesses and how it's handled, see the Privacy Policy.
